Who we are

ShareGNSS is an independent Android app published under the developer name ShareGNSS. This documentation site is maintained under the fa1keh.soft project name.

For privacy questions or requests, contact fa1keh.soft@gmail.com.

The app does not operate a location server, create a ShareGNSS account, show advertisements, or keep a saved location history. Location sharing goes from your phone to the selected, paired laptop. Google Play, email support, and website hosting involve the separate services described below.

Location and Bluetooth sharing

While sharing is active, ShareGNSS uses Android’s GNSS interfaces to read precise location, satellite status, accuracy, position timing, and native NMEA sentences. It uses this information to display reception and decide whether a satellite fix is fresh and usable.

The app transmits NMEA GGA data over Bluetooth Classic to the laptop you select and pair. A valid sentence contains latitude, longitude, altitude above mean sea level, satellite/fix-quality information, and UTC fix time. The receiving software, such as N.I.N.A., may retain the imported location under its own settings and data practices.

ShareGNSS does not send your GNSS position to the developer or include it in Google Play billing requests. It checks the connecting Bluetooth device against your selected laptop.

You start sharing yourself, or choose Start when app opens with a remembered laptop. An active sharing session can continue while the phone is locked. Use Stop sharing in the app or its notification to end the session. Sharing does not start automatically at device boot.

Location and recent diagnostic data are held in the app’s running process, rather than written to a location-history file. Recent session information, including the last transmitted NMEA sentence, can remain in memory until reset or the app process ends.

Data stored on your phone

ShareGNSS stores the following in its private Android app storage:

  • Laptop selection: the selected laptop’s Bluetooth address and name when Remember last laptop is enabled.
  • Preferences: automatic startup, appearance, and notification-prompt state.
  • Trial timing: whether the trial has started, remaining time, timestamps, and a device boot counter used to calculate elapsed trial time.
  • Purchase evidence: signed Google Play purchase data, its signature, and acknowledgement information used to verify access and allow previously verified use offline. This can include a purchase token, order identifier, product, purchase time, and purchase state.

These records do not contain a saved GNSS location history. They remain until you change the relevant setting, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it. Purchase evidence can also be removed after Google Play confirms that the purchase is no longer owned. App preferences are excluded from the app’s configured Android cloud backup and device-transfer rules.

Google Play purchases

ShareGNSS uses Google Play Billing to display your local price, make a one-time purchase, check ownership, acknowledge a completed purchase, and restore access. These operations communicate with Google Play and require an internet connection.

Google processes the payment and associated account, device, and transaction information under its own policies. The app receives purchase results and signed evidence; it does not receive your payment-card number. Order information made available to the publisher by Play Console may be used to handle purchase questions, refunds, and related records.

ShareGNSS verifies purchase evidence on the phone. It does not send it to a developer-operated purchase server. Previously verified access can work offline; Google Play may reconcile ownership when the app next connects.

Read the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s processing and retention practices.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request, investigate the reported issue, and maintain necessary support or purchase records. Email is handled through Gmail, a Google service.

Send only information needed to explain the problem. In particular, remove precise coordinates, Bluetooth addresses, purchase tokens, and private device names from screenshots or diagnostic text unless they are specifically needed for your request.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request or meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence using the contact address above. Any records that must be retained will be limited to that purpose.

This documentation website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its processing and retention are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We have not added analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, sign-in forms, or third-party video embeds to these pages. Images and styles are served with the site. Following an external link or sending an email takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Your controls and deletion

  • Stop sharing ends GNSS updates and the app’s Bluetooth sharing session.
  • Disable Remember last laptop to remove the saved laptop name and address from persistent settings. The current in-memory selection may remain until the app process ends.
  • Revoke location or Nearby devices permissions in Android settings to prevent the corresponding app access.
  • Clear ShareGNSS storage or uninstall it to remove its local settings, trial metadata, and cached purchase evidence. Clearing data does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. A valid purchase can be restored through the same Google Play account.
  • Remove imported locations on your laptop through the receiving application’s settings. ShareGNSS cannot delete data already retained by other software.
  • Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the privacy contact above.

ShareGNSS has no developer-hosted user account to delete. Your Google account and Google Play records are managed separately through Google.

Security and data use

The app uses Android’s private app storage for preferences and purchase evidence. Bluetooth sharing uses Android’s paired RFCOMM connection and permits only the selected laptop. Permission checks control access to precise location and Bluetooth. Keep control of your paired devices and stop sharing when you are finished.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or profiling. ShareGNSS includes no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Android, Google Play, GitHub, and email services may process service or diagnostic data under their own policies.

For information you voluntarily send us, contact the privacy address to request access, correction, or deletion. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Where applicable, you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Policy updates

We will update this page if ShareGNSS’s data handling changes and revise the date above. Material changes that require additional notice or permission will be communicated through the app or its release information as appropriate.
