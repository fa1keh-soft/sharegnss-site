ShareGNSS / App review

Foreground service demonstration

A real Pixel recording showing laptop selection, starting sharing, a Bluetooth connection, and stopping from the ongoing notification.

Open or download the video · 40 seconds · no sign-in required

What the recording shows

  1. Choose the already paired Windows laptop.
  2. Tap Start sharing. GNSS reception and the Bluetooth listener start.
  3. The laptop opens its outgoing COM port and receives NMEA GGA messages.
  4. The ongoing ShareGNSS notification provides a Stop sharing action.
  5. Stop sharing from the notification. The app confirms Location paused and Sharing is off.

This indoor control-flow demonstration transmits no-fix status. It does not claim a valid satellite position or a N.I.N.A. location import. The notification sequence was captured in a second session using the same app version, 0.3.5 (10). Build 0.3.6 (11) adds complimentary access codes without changing this service behavior.

Private device names and addresses are obscured. Position details are collapsed. The status bar, other notifications, and the transition back to the app are concealed. Original audio is excluded.
